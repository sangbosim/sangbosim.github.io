01ABOUT

RESEARCH PROFILE

From numerical methods to operational flood forecasting.

I am Sang-Bo Sim, a researcher at the Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology (KICT), working at the intersection of urban hydraulics, computational modeling, and high-performance computing.

My work centers on coupling EPA-SWMM's 1D sewer hydraulics with 2D surface flow solvers, validating hydraulic accuracy, and making physically based simulations fast enough for practical forecasting.

PRIMARY EXPERTISE
  • 1D–2D dual-drainage modeling
  • Finite-volume hydrodynamics
  • GPU computing & parallelization
  • Numerical accuracy & stability
  • Real-time urban flood prediction

01 / CORE MODEL↗

HC-SURF / KICT-UF

Integrated 1D–2D urban flood simulation

Physics-based sewer–surface coupling that links dynamic-wave pipe flows with a finite-volume 2D surface solver. Key topics include manhole exchange, rainfall representation, synchronization, validation, and network simplification.
