01ABOUT

RESEARCH PROFILE

From numerical methods to operational flood forecasting.

I am Sang-Bo Sim, a researcher at the Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology (KICT), working at the intersection of urban hydraulics, computational modeling, and high-performance computing.

My research develops KICT-UF (dynamic-wave 2D hydraulics) and KICT-UF Lite (diffusive-wave 2D hydraulics). Both couple SWMM dynamic-wave sewer routing with surface flow, while supporting different rainfall–runoff pathways and operational objectives.

PRIMARY EXPERTISE
  • 1D–2D dual-drainage modeling
  • Finite-volume hydrodynamics
  • GPU computing & parallelization
  • Numerical accuracy & stability
  • Real-time urban flood prediction

02MODELS & RESEARCH

Two models. One integrated urban flood framework.

KICT-UF and KICT-UF Lite share 1D dynamic-wave sewer hydraulics and two-way sewer–surface exchange, while differing in 2D governing equations and rainfall–runoff pathways.
